Why Charitree Exists

Closing the Trust Gap in Grassroots Giving

Too many local charities do life-changing work yet lack the tools to show supporters where funds go after checkout. Charitree was founded in 2023 by community organizers, educators, and civic technologists to bridge that gap.

Instead of pooling money into opaque general budgets, each Charitree campaign breaks down exact equipment, field labor, and logistics costs upfront. Local field coordinators upload milestone photos, supplier invoices, and audit summaries directly to our public ledger.

  • 2023

    Seed Launch & Pilot Classrooms

    Equipped 6 rural learning centers with solar lighting and bilingual STEM kits for 940 students.

  • 2024

    Open Ledger Standard

    Introduced public category-level disbursement tracking and independent quarterly reviews.

  • 2025–2026

    Regional Water & Canopy Network

    Scaled to 18 active districts covering clean water filtration, mobile clinics, and native reforestation.

Our Four Operating Principles

Every partner organization signs onto these non-negotiable standards before listing a campaign:

  • Radical Clarity: Publish target budgets, vendor quotes, and administrative caps before raising a single dollar.
  • Local Leadership: Fund community-led teams who know regional needs firsthand.
  • Dignity First: Document impact through verified outcomes while respecting beneficiary privacy.
  • Independent Verification: Cross-check disbursements with receipts and external field audits.
